# Disclaimer

Garmin Coach is **experimental software**. It is not a medical device and it is not medical or professional coaching advice.

- **No promise of results.** The plans are drafts built from your own data and general training conventions. Nothing here predicts how much you will improve or by when.
- **Evidence labels are honest, not a guarantee.** Each rule is marked *research* (a paper in the library supports the direction), *practice* (a convention or a choice of this project) or *gap* (nothing read supports it). Many studies are small, use young or trained athletes, and may not apply to you.
- **Pain, dizziness, chest pain, fainting or a changed gait: stop and see a clinician.** A readiness colour or a plan never overrides how you feel.
- **Devices:** the workout `.fit` files and Zone targets have not been verified on every Garmin watch. Check each workout on your watch before you rely on it.
- **Heart-rate numbers** from a watch (max HR, LTHR) are estimates; published studies show errors of around 10 bpm for LTHR. The planner labels them as estimates.
